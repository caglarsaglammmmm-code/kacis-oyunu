TITLE:
Drift Lab: Police Escape

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Drift through tight streets, outrun the police, grow your garage. Plays offline.

PROMO TEXT:
New: historic squares, two stadiums, a giant college campus and 6 languages!

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Welcome to Drift Lab: the car drives itself, you just steer.

Tap the right or left side of the screen, throw it into the corner and burn some rubber. The police are on your tail, the streets are tight and the traffic is heavy. The longer you survive, the more score and coins you earn.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Three control styles: Left / Right taps, Wheel or Joystick
• A real drift feel at high speed
• An endless city: neighborhoods, bazaars, roundabouts, a highway, beaches, forests and deserts
• Historic city landmarks: a green-domed museum, a mosque on a hill, tram stops, a covered bazaar and vineyard houses
• The biggest building on the map: Mandalina College and its orange campus
• Police pressure that keeps rising: chasers, pincer moves, head-on interceptors, roadblocks, barricades, SWAT and tanks
• A surprise alien invasion if you last long enough
• Ten original vehicles, each with four paint jobs
• Blast, Ghost, Repair and Giant power-ups
• Day and night cycle, glowing signs, living streets
• English, Turkish, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish and German
• Plays in landscape and portrait

No internet needed. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

All brand, store, school, stadium, place and vehicle names in the game are fictional and not affiliated with any real person, organization or brand. Buildings are shown in a general, stylized way.
